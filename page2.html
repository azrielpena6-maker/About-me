All about me page 2

One of my all-time favorite animals is the hammerhead shark. I love how unique they look, especially their wide, hammer-shaped heads. They’re fascinating creatures, and I enjoy learning more about them whenever I can. I have three siblings: two brothers and one sister. They make life fun and sometimes challenging, but I wouldn’t trade them for anything. We like to spend time together, and they’re a big part of my life. My favorite color is purple, and my two favorite foods are fufu and goat soup, and jamaican beef patties.
    My favorite places I have traveled to: Missouri – I went to Missouri on a field trip to the George Washington Carver museum but the bus ride there with my friends, and the gift shop were the most fun parts of the trip. New York – I actually went to New York for a funeral but my family down there made it less sad and gloomy and they made it easier to accept the loss that we had. while I was there I went to times square and had lots of fun and I saw somebody do a backflip over 4 people. Texas – I go to Texas every summer and Christmas to visit family and I always have lots of fun.
